# A Digital Picture Frame

Another thing I wanted to do was to try mimicking web designs and animations I find on the internet everywhere and catalog them somewhere. That is what the new [Web Designs](/web-designs) page is for. The sources are there too in each of the web designs, so you can use them as reference too.

I wanted to do this because I have some weird habit of downloading images of 'cool', 'retro', 'futuristic', 'cyberpunky', 'cosmos' things on my phone, or taking pictures of designs I like while I'm on my way to work or traveling. It has become a hobby. But the reason I do those is to try animating them and displaying them somewhere. Since I can't have those digital animations in a picture frame hanging off the wall at home, I want to do it on this personal website.

So far I only have a few completed and will be constantly updating here.

For some reason, these animations are just pleasing to look at, aesthetically calming, and they have this sense of nostalgic remembrance somewhere.
